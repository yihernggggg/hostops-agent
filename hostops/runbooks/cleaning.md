# Cleaning

Every turnover is done by SparkleClean with a photo checklist.

- Dirty on arrival, missing towels or linen: apologise and dispatch SparkleClean for a touch-up
  (needs host approval, it costs a call-out fee).
- Pests (ants, cockroaches): dispatch pest control via the host.
- Toilet paper and basics are in the storeroom; tell the guest where before dispatching anyone.
