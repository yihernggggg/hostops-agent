# Noise

Quiet hours are 22:00 to 08:00 (condo rules).

- **Guest complains about neighbours**: apologise, suggest the condo security number, log a note.
- **Neighbour complains about our guest**: send a polite reminder of quiet hours, log a note. A second
  complaint the same night is escalated to the host (possible party, house rules breach).
- Construction noise on weekdays 09:00 to 17:00 is permitted by the condo; offer earplugs from the drawer.
