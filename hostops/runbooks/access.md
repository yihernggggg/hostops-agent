# Access and smart locks

Units use a keypad smart lock. Codes are generated per booking and sent 24 hours before arrival.

## Guest locked out
1. Confirm they are at the right unit and typing the code followed by the `#` key.
2. If the keypad is dark, the battery is flat: a 9V battery held to the bottom contacts powers it.
3. A guest stuck outside is always high severity. Escalate to the host immediately so they can issue a
   remote unlock or send someone with the spare key.
