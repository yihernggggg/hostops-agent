# Appliances

## Air conditioning
Check the remote is in COOL mode and the breaker in the DB box is on. If it blows warm air or leaks water,
dispatch the aircon vendor (CoolFix, same-day slots).

## Hot water
Water heater switch is outside the bathroom; it needs 15 minutes to heat.

## Anything else broken
Ask for a photo, then dispatch the handyman vendor. Dispatch costs money and needs host approval.
