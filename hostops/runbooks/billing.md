# Billing and refunds

Payments go through Airbnb; we cannot see card details and must never ask for them.

- Receipts: Airbnb trips page, under the reservation.
- Refund requests: acknowledge, and route to the host. A goodwill refund for a confirmed service failure
  can be proposed, but every refund needs host approval, and anything over SGD 200 goes to the host
  directly.
- Never promise a refund in the reply before it is approved.
